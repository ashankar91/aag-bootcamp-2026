Applications for the AAG Bootcamp 2026

How to Apply

The bootcamp is aimed at graduate students in mathematics with an interest in arithmetic or algebraic geometry. Participants at all stages of graduate study are encouraged to apply; no specific prerequisites beyond a solid foundation in algebra and algebraic geometry are assumed.

Applications are reviewed by the organizers. Decisions will be communicated by email. We expect to be able to support travel and local expenses for a number of participants — please indicate your funding situation in the application form.

Applications are submitted via Google Form.

Application Deadline

Application deadline to be announced.

What the Application Asks

The application form will ask for:

  • Name, institution, and year in graduate school
  • Advisor name
  • Brief description of mathematical background and research interests
  • Why you would like to attend
  • Whether you are requesting financial support
  • Gender (used for roommate matching)
  • Roommate preferences (specific request, or gender preference if no specific request)

The exact fields may be adjusted; the form above will reflect the final version.
